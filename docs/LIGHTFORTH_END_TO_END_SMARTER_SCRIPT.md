In this series, I’m going to walk you through, step by step, how to use the new Lightforth throughout your job search — from building and tailoring your resume, to finding and applying for jobs, preparing for interviews, and even having a Copilot with you when it’s time for the actual interview.

The goal is simple: **help you spend less time struggling with the job-search process and give you a better chance of landing your next role.**

Think of Lightforth as your AI career buddy.

It helps you get jobs faster, whether that means building a stronger resume, finding more relevant jobs, preparing before the interview, or helping you answer better during the interview itself.

What could have taken you months of manual effort, Lightforth helps you compress into a much shorter, smarter process.

So, let’s get started.

To create your account, go to the new Lightforth app and click **Get Started**.

Enter your details, create your account, and once you're in, you'll land on your Lightforth dashboard.

As a free user, Lightforth gives you **five free credits every month** to get started. That should be enough for us to explore some of the major things you can do on the platform.

In the next video, we're starting with one of the most important parts of any job application:

**Your resume.**

Let me show you how to build and tailor one with Lightforth.

## Episode 2 — How to Use Lightforth Resume Builder

Alright, let's start with the Resume Builder.

One of the easiest ways to get started on Lightforth is by uploading a resume you already have.

From your dashboard, click **Tailor my Resume**, or go to the resume section and click **Upload a Resume**.

If you've already created resumes on Lightforth, you can also choose **Lightforth Resume** and select an existing one.

But for this tutorial, I'm going to upload an existing resume from my computer.

Once I upload it, Lightforth starts parsing the resume.

It's basically reading the document and extracting important information about me — my experience, skills, professional summary, job role, education, and other relevant information.

Give it a moment to finish.

And there we go.

Now that Lightforth understands my resume, you'll notice that I have four different things I can do with it.

I can **tailor my resume**, **practice for an interview**, **use it with Interview Copilot**, or **use it for Auto Apply**.

We'll cover the other options later in this series.

For now, click **Tailor My Resume**.

### The Resume Canvas

This takes us into the Lightforth Resume Builder.

Let me quickly show you around before we start editing.

On the left-hand side, you'll see three main sections:

**Chat, Create, and Template.**

And at the top, you'll find options to preview your resume, rename it, check your ATS score, and download it when you're finished.

But here's where things get interesting.

You don't have to manually rewrite everything on your resume.

**You can simply talk to Lightforth.**

### Tailoring Your Resume to a Job

Let's say I found a job that I really want to apply for.

Instead of spending the next hour manually changing my resume, I'm going to copy the **job description** from the job posting.

Then I'll come back to Lightforth and paste it directly into the chat.

Lightforth will analyze the job description, compare it with the information already available in my resume, and suggest changes that make my resume more relevant to this particular role.

And there we go.

You can see the proposed changes directly on the resume.

The important thing here is that **you're still in control**.

You can review what Lightforth changed and choose to **accept or reject the suggestions**.

If you're happy with everything, you can accept all the changes.

If you're not satisfied, reject them or continue chatting with the AI.

You could say:

> Make my professional summary shorter.

Or:

> Emphasize my product management experience.

Or:

You can continue going back and forth until you're satisfied with the result.

That's what makes the Resume Builder useful.

You're not starting from scratch every time you find another job.

You already have your career information inside Lightforth, and you can use AI to adapt your resume for the specific opportunity you're applying for.

Once you're satisfied with the resume, you're ready to download it.

Click **Download**.

## Episode 3 — How to Find & Apply for Jobs with Lightforth Auto Apply

Hi, welcome back.

My name is Moski. I’m a Product Manager at Lightforth, and in this video, I’ll show you how to use Lightforth Auto Apply AI to find jobs and apply for them.

So, let’s dive right in.

Once you’ve signed up or logged into Lightforth, go to the left side of your dashboard and click Auto Apply.

The first thing Lightforth will ask you to do is set up your job preferences.

### Step 1 — Upload Your Resume

The first step is to upload your resume.

Once you’ve uploaded it, Lightforth will parse your resume and extract important information about you, including your professional summary, work experience, skills, and other relevant details.

Once that’s done, click Next.

### Step 2 — Add Your Contact Information

The next step is your contact information.

This is important because Lightforth needs this information when applying to jobs on your behalf.

You’ll provide things like your email address, phone number, location, postal code, and other contact information.

There’s a good chance Lightforth has already extracted some of this information from your resume.

But I recommend going through everything one more time to make sure it’s correct and making any adjustments where necessary.

Once everything looks good, click Next.

### Step 3 — Set Your Job Preferences

Now, this part is very important because you’re basically telling Lightforth what kind of jobs you want it to find for you.

Start by selecting your preferred job roles.

You can select between one and five different roles that you’re interested in.

Then set your other preferences, including your expected salary, employment type, preferred work arrangement, location, visa or work authorization information, and the other options available to you.

For location, you can also select up to five preferred locations.

Take your time here.

The better your preferences are, the better Lightforth can understand what kind of opportunities it should be looking for.

Once you’re satisfied, click Next.

### Step 4 — Additional Information

Next, Lightforth will ask you for some additional information.

This is information the AI may need when it eventually gets to an employer’s application form.

You’ll be asked to confirm things like your work experience and answer other application-related questions.

You’ll also see optional demographic and equal-opportunity questions that employers may include on their applications.

Fill in the information you’re comfortable providing and make sure your answers are accurate.

The reason we’re doing all of this now is simple:

We want Lightforth to already know what to say when it encounters these questions during an application.

That means fewer interruptions for you later.

Once you’re done, click **Continue**, review your job preferences one more time, and click **Save & Continue**.

Now your Auto Apply profile is ready.

### Meet Your Auto Apply Agents

Now this is where Auto Apply gets interesting.

Lightforth uses a set of AI agents to handle different parts of your job search and application process.

You’ll see agents including Scout, Filter, Tailor, and Driver.

Let me explain what each of them does.

### Scout — Finds the Jobs

First, we have Scout.

Scout goes out and finds job opportunities based on the preferences you just created.

Instead of you constantly jumping between different job boards looking for opportunities, Scout does the searching for you.

It looks across supported job sources for opportunities that could be relevant to you.

Once Scout finds those jobs, they’re passed to the next agent.

### Filter — Finds the Right Jobs

The second agent is Filter.

Finding jobs isn’t enough.

We need to know whether those jobs actually make sense for you.

So Filter takes the opportunities Scout has discovered and scores them against the preferences you created earlier.

Your role.

Your location.

Your experience.

Your work preferences.

And the other information you’ve provided.

Jobs that aren’t relevant are filtered out.

The better matches are then presented to you.

### Your Jobs

Now go to the Jobs tab.

This is where you’ll see the opportunities Lightforth has found and filtered for you.

You can go through them, review the jobs you’re interested in, and when you find one you want Lightforth to apply to, simply click Apply.

Find another one you like?

Click Apply.

And another?

Click Apply.

You don’t have to start filling out every application yourself.

Once you’ve selected the jobs you want, the rest of the Auto Apply system starts working.

### Tailor — Prepares Your Resume

Before Lightforth submits your application, Tailor checks your resume against the job description.

If your existing resume is already a strong match, great.

But if it isn’t, Tailor can create a tailored version of your resume that better matches the opportunity you’re applying for.

So you’re not necessarily sending the exact same generic resume to every company.

Lightforth can prepare a resume that’s more relevant to that particular job.

Once everything is ready, the application moves to Driver.

### Driver — Applies for You

Driver handles the actual application process.

It goes through the application flow, fills in the required information, answers questions using the information you’ve already provided, attaches the appropriate resume, and works through the application process for you.

And while all of this is happening, you can track what’s going on from Lightforth.

### Tracking Your Applications

Go to the Applied tab.

This is where you’ll see the jobs Lightforth has attempted or completed applications for.

You’ll also see the status of each application.

It could be In Progress, meaning Lightforth is still working on it.

It could be Success, meaning the application has been completed.

It could be Failed.

Or it could be Needs Review.

Needs Review is particularly important.

Sometimes an employer asks a question that Lightforth doesn’t have enough information to answer confidently.

Instead of guessing on your behalf, the application can be flagged for you to review.

You can provide the missing information and continue from there.

So I recommend checking your Applied tab regularly to see if anything needs your attention.

### Reviewing What Was Submitted

For your applications, Lightforth also keeps the important information you may need later.

You’ll be able to see things like the job description, job link, resume used for the application, application status, and timeline of the process.

Where available, Lightforth can also provide screenshots or recordings from the application process.

This becomes especially useful if a recruiter reaches out to you weeks later.

Instead of thinking:

“Wait, which job was this again?”

You can come back to Lightforth, find the application, review the job description, see the resume you applied with, and prepare for the conversation.

And that’s basically how Lightforth Auto Apply works.

You tell Lightforth what you’re looking for.

Scout finds the opportunities.

Filter finds the ones that fit you.

Tailor prepares your resume.

And Driver handles the application.

You remain in control of which jobs you want to apply for and can track everything from your Lightforth dashboard.

In the next episode, we’re moving to the next stage of the job-search journey.

You applied for the job. Now imagine they call you back for an interview.

I’ll show you how to use Lightforth Interview Prep to practice and prepare before the real thing.

See you in the next one.

## Episode 4 — How to Prepare for an Interview with Lightforth Interview Prep

Hey, welcome back.

I’m Moski, a Product Manager at Lightforth, and welcome to Lightforth AI — your AI career companion designed to help you land your next job or role.

In this class, I’m going to show you how to use Lightforth Interview Prep to practice for an interview before the real thing.

So, let’s get started.

### Getting Started

From your Lightforth dashboard, go to the menu on the left-hand side and click Interview Prep.

There’s also another way to get here.

From your dashboard, you can upload your resume and select Practice for Interview.

So, what exactly is Interview Prep?

Interview Prep is basically an AI interview simulator.

It acts like a real interviewer, asks you questions based on the role you’re preparing for, listens to your responses, and evaluates how well you answer.

At the end of the session, Lightforth gives you a report showing how you performed and where you need to improve.

The idea is pretty simple:

Don’t let the real interview be the first time you’re answering those questions.

Practice here first.

### Setting Up Your Interview

On the Interview Prep page, upload a resume or choose an existing Lightforth resume.

Then Lightforth will ask you to configure the interview.

The first thing Lightforth needs to know is what you’re preparing for.

Enter the job role you’re interviewing for.

You can also provide additional information about the opportunity, such as the company name.

Confirm the resume you want to use for this practice session.

This is important because we want the interviewer to understand your actual experience and background.

You can also paste the job description.

The more relevant context you provide, the better Lightforth can tailor the interview to the actual opportunity you’re preparing for.

Once you’re done, click Continue.

### Choose Your Interview Preferences

Next, you can customize the type of interview you want to practice.

For example, you can choose the interview stage or type you’re preparing for, such as an introductory interview, technical interview, or another stage of the hiring process.

You can also choose your preferred difficulty and interview style.

Maybe you’re just getting started and want something beginner-friendly.

Or maybe you want the interviewer to be more difficult and strict with you.

Choose whatever best represents the interview you’re preparing for.

Once you’re happy with your setup, continue.

### Choose Your Interviewer

Next, choose your interviewer.

You’ll see different voice options available, so simply select whichever interviewer you’re most comfortable practicing with.

Read through the final instructions, make sure your microphone is working, and when you’re ready, start your interview.

### Taking the Interview

Now, from this point, I want you to treat this like a real interview.

The AI interviewer will start asking you questions.

Listen to the question and answer it exactly the way you would if you were speaking to an actual recruiter or hiring manager.

Don’t rush through it just because it’s AI.

If the interviewer asks:

“Tell me about yourself.”

Answer properly.

If you’re asked about your experience, explain it.

If you’re asked a difficult question, try to work through it.

Remember, the whole point of practicing is to discover where you’re struggling before the real interview.

While you’re speaking, Lightforth also creates a transcript of the conversation.

You’ll be able to see the questions you were asked and your responses, so you can reference them later.

Continue answering the questions until you’re satisfied with the practice session.

### Your Interview Report

When you’re done, click End Session.

Lightforth will then analyze your interview session and generate your performance report.

Depending on the length of your interview, this may take a little time to process.

You don’t have to sit on the page waiting for it.

If the report isn’t immediately available, Lightforth will send you an email notification when it’s ready.

Then you can come back and review your results.

You’ll be able to see how you performed and, most importantly, identify the areas where you need to improve before your actual interview.

And that’s how you use Lightforth Interview Prep.

You can practice as many times as you need to until you become more comfortable answering interview questions.

Because when the actual interview comes, you don’t want to be figuring out your answers for the first time.

You want to already be prepared.

Now, we’ve built the resume.

We’ve found and applied for jobs.

And we’ve practiced for the interview.

But what happens when it’s time for the actual live interview?

That’s what we’re covering next.

In the next class, I’ll show you how to use Lightforth Interview Copilot during a live interview.

## Episode 5 — How to Use Lightforth Interview Copilot

Hi, welcome back.

My name is Moski, I’m a Product Manager at Lightforth, and welcome to Lightforth AI.

So far, we’ve built our resume, applied for jobs, and practiced for interviews.

Now let’s talk about the last feature I want to show you in this series: Interview Copilot.

Interview Copilot helps you answer questions better during a live interview. It listens to the interviewer, understands the question being asked, and provides suggested answers on your screen in real time.

There are two versions of Lightforth Interview Copilot:

The Web Copilot and the Desktop Copilot.

I’m going to show you how to use both, and when you might want to use one over the other.

Let’s start with the web version.

### Using the Web Copilot

From your Lightforth dashboard, you can upload your resume and select Interview Copilot.

Alternatively, go to the menu on the left-hand side and click Interview Copilot.

From here, upload a resume or choose an existing Lightforth resume.

The first thing you need to do is provide Lightforth with some context about the interview you’re about to have.

Enter your job role, confirm the resume you used for the application, and paste the job description.

I recommend setting this up about 15 to 20 minutes before your interview.

You don’t want to be doing all of this when your interviewer is already waiting for you.

Once you’ve added everything, click Continue.

### Choose How You Want Lightforth to Help You

Next, you can choose your response preference.

You have three options:

Default, Headlines, and Coaching.

Let’s quickly go through them.

Default gives you a more complete suggested response. If you want Lightforth to help you formulate what you could say, this is the option for you.

Headlines gives you the important points and structure you should cover in your answer.

So instead of giving you everything word for word, it can help you remember the key points you need to talk about.

For behavioural questions, for example, this could help you structure your response around things like the situation, task, action, and result.

And then there’s Coaching.

Coaching focuses more on guiding you through what to say and how to approach your response, rather than simply giving you an answer.

Choose whichever style works best for you.

You can also choose your preferred answer length: Short, Medium, or Long.

Once you’re done, click Continue.

### Setting Up Your Interview Audio

Now you’ll land on the Interview Copilot canvas.

If your interview is happening on Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another supported platform, join the interview a few minutes early.

Back on Lightforth, click the option to share your interview screen or audio.

Depending on where your interview is happening, you may be able to share a browser tab, application window, or your screen.

The important thing is to make sure you’re sharing the audio from the interview.

If Lightforth can’t receive the interview audio, Copilot can’t hear the questions being asked.

Once everything is connected correctly, you’ll see your interview inside the Copilot experience, so you don’t have to constantly jump between different windows.

When you’re ready, click Start Interview.

Lightforth will let you know when the audio has been captured and Copilot is ready to listen.

Now you’re ready for your interview.

### During Your Live Interview

Once the interviewer asks a question, Lightforth listens to it and provides a suggested response.

But here’s something important:

Don’t just read everything word for word.

Read the suggestion, understand the important points, and answer the interviewer naturally in your own words.

Think of Copilot as assistance.

Maybe you know the answer but you’re nervous.

Maybe there’s an important example from your experience that you’re struggling to remember.

Or maybe you simply need help structuring your response.

That’s where Copilot comes in.

You’re still the person having the interview.

### Copilot Controls

During your interview, you’ll also see a Settings option.

From here, you can customize how Copilot behaves.

For example, you can change the font size depending on how much information you want to see on your screen.

You can adjust the scroll behaviour so answers are easier for you to follow.

You can also choose between Automatic and Manual response modes.

In Automatic mode, Lightforth automatically detects questions and generates responses.

Manual mode gives you more control.

This can be useful when an interviewer is speaking for a long time, thinking aloud, or having a normal conversation and you don’t necessarily want Copilot generating a response to everything they say.

With Manual mode, you can configure a key that tells Lightforth when you actually want an answer.

Choose whichever setup feels most comfortable for you.

### After Your Interview

Once your interview is finished, click End Session.

Lightforth keeps the transcript from your session so you can go back and review the questions that were asked and how the conversation went.

And that’s basically how the Web Copilot works.

Now let me show you the Desktop Copilot.

### Using the Desktop Copilot

The Desktop Copilot gives you a dedicated Lightforth application that lives directly on your computer.

To get it, go to Download App from your Lightforth menu.

You’ll see the available versions depending on your operating system.

If you’re using Windows, download the Windows version.

If you’re using a Mac, you’ll need to choose the correct version for your Mac: Intel or Apple Silicon.

If you don’t know which one you have, click the Apple icon at the top-left corner of your Mac and select About This Mac.

You’ll be able to see which processor your Mac uses.

Download the appropriate version and install it.

### Setting Up on Mac

On a Mac, Lightforth may require some system permissions before everything can work correctly.

After moving Lightforth into your Applications folder and opening it, follow the instructions you’re shown to grant the required permissions.

Your Mac may ask you for your password or biometrics to approve these changes.

Make sure all the required permissions show as enabled before continuing.

If something isn’t enabled, follow the setup instructions before trying to start an interview.

For Windows users, the setup is generally more straightforward. Install the application and follow the instructions you’re shown.

### Starting a Desktop Copilot Session

Once the application is installed, sign in with your Lightforth account.

The setup should already feel familiar.

Enter the job you’re interviewing for, add your job description, and select the resume you want Copilot to use.

Then choose your response preference and answer length.

Once you’re done, continue to the interview.

One of the advantages of the desktop application is that it can capture the required interview audio directly from your device, so you don’t have to go through the same browser-sharing flow every time.

Set everything up a few minutes before your interview and start Copilot when you’re ready.

### Desktop Controls

The Desktop Copilot also gives you additional controls over how the application behaves.

From Settings, you can adjust things like your font size, scrolling behaviour, answer mode, and window preferences.

You can decide where you want Lightforth positioned on your screen and configure the experience so that it’s comfortable for you during the interview.

Lightforth also provides privacy-focused display controls for supported environments. Make sure you understand and test your setup before the actual interview rather than discovering how your configuration works in the middle of a call.

And that’s it.

That’s how you use Lightforth Interview Copilot on both web and desktop.

We want Lightforth to be your AI career companion throughout the journey — from the moment you start looking for opportunities until you’re ready to land your next role.
